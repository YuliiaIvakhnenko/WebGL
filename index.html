Drop Surface with Textures

Drag your mouse on the drop to rotate it.
(On a touch screen, you can use your finger.)

 
Texture

Texture Transform (Even variant: Rotation)
Move Pivot (u,v): W/A/S/D (Shift = faster).
While you move the pivot, the texture rotation is recalculated around this point. You can also fine-tune the angle with the slider.


 
Center UV: 0.50, 0.50
W/S moves V, A/D moves U (hold Shift for faster move). The pivot wraps in [0..1] (it never stops at the border). Rotation is always applied around the current pivot.
